NEON BAY

Twelve blocks of bad decisions.

On foot

  • WASD move
  • Shift sprint
  • Mouse aim · Click shoot
  • F enter / exit vehicle
  • 1–5 or QE weapons

Driving

  • WS throttle / brake
  • AD steer
  • Space handbrake (drift)
  • Click drive-by

World

  • H start job / side hustle
  • M full map
  • R change radio station
  • P or Esc pause

Yellow marker = next story job. Steal a taxi or a cop car for side work. Progress saves automatically.
